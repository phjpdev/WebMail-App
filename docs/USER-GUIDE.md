# D&J Webmail — User Guide (1 page)

## Logging in

Open your webmail URL in a browser. Enter your username and password provided by your administrator.

## Reading mail

- Use the **folder list** on the left to browse folders (Inbox, Support, Sent, etc.)
- Click a message to read it
- Unread messages appear in **bold**

## Organizing mail

When you log in, the system **automatically sorts new Inbox mail** into the correct folders (spam, employee, client folders). Mail is only sorted while someone has the webmail open — if no one logs in overnight, sorting happens on the first login the next day.

## Replying

1. Open a message
2. Click **Reply**
3. The **Send as** address defaults to the alias the email was received on — leave it unless instructed otherwise
4. Click **Send**

## Composing new mail

1. Click **Compose**
2. Choose **Send as** identity
3. Fill in To, Subject, and message
4. Click **Send**

## Moving and deleting

- **Move to…** — move a message to another folder
- **Delete** — moves the message to Trash

## Need help?

Contact your administrator to add folders, aliases, or filter rules.
